Little robot lab

Move the blue robot. Push the orange puck into the green target.
Explore one action at a time, then save what happened.

Blue: robot · Orange: movable puck · Green ring: goal

No trained model is connected.

Arrow keys work when the canvas is focused. Reset keeps earlier recordings in memory. Recording stops at 500 transitions; save the trace before closing the tab.

What are we learning?

This is a deliberately simple 2D, contact-based world. Each recorded action captures a 64 × 64 image before and after the move, plus the exact state and goal distance. A JEPA could learn to predict future representations from images and actions; a language-model controller could choose from the five discrete actions. Neither is implemented yet.

Motion is kinematic: there is no inertia, friction or real robot dynamics. The random policy is a baseline, not an AI demo. Inspired by reading LeWAM; this is not a reproduction of its benchmark.
